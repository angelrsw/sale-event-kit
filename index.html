BEHIND THE KIT

Your details become
a ready-to-use kit.

  1. Start with a designed template.

    Choose from six layouts and ten fonts. Add your logo, a photo, and icons. Your details fill the print, social, and website versions.

  2. Review the writing.

    Prepared writing templates organize what you enter. You can edit the announcement and team checklist before downloading.

  3. Download one ZIP file.

    Unzip it to get print and social assets, website HTML/CSS, announcements, a team guide, and your editable project.

No Canva, Adobe, or AI connection is needed here.

This preview creates the files in your browser. Images are finished graphics; make changes in the builder and download again. The print PDF is for ordinary office printing, with no commercial bleed or CMYK setup.

A launched version could add an email signup and CRM connection. This preview does not collect leads or send anything to a service.
